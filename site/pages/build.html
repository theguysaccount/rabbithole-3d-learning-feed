Good to know

  • Only embeddable videos work. The builder asks YouTube about each link and refuses videos whose owners don't allow embedding.
  • Nothing is uploaded. The path lives in your browser and in the link you share. Anyone with the link can watch it; to change it, edit and share a new link.
  • Keep the main track short. Five to eight clips is plenty. Put background, maths and examples in deeper tracks so viewers choose them.
  • Use start and end times to cut a long video down to the minute that answers the question.
