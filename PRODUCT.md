# PRODUCT

**User.** Educators and course creators first; then creators with deep back catalogues, founders pitching, and teams making onboarding or help videos. Viewers: learners who like short videos but want structure and depth on demand.

**Painful job.** Turning a pile of good videos into something people can follow at their own depth. Playlists force one order and one depth; interactive-video platforms want uploads, editing and a subscription.

**Promise.** Paste YouTube links, arrange a main track with side paths that go deeper, share one link. Viewers swipe down to continue and sideways to dig in. Free, no account.

**Wedge.** Zero setup on content that already exists: any embeddable YouTube video, trimmed with start and end times, arranged in minutes. The share link is the product (no hosting), and every shared path carries a "make your own" route back to the builder.

**Why now.** Short-video habits (swipe to continue) are universal, and the best explanations of most topics are already on YouTube in pieces. Nobody has made "swipe deeper" a standard way to navigate them.

**Activation metric.** `hole_shared`: a creator copied or shared a rabbit-hole link (counted cookielessly once key 2 is set). Supporting events: `clip_added`, `go_deeper` (viewers actually use the depth), `embed_copied`.

**First revenue event.** A creator pays for the tier the static site can't provide: hosted paths with short links that stay editable after sharing, path analytics (which branches people take), private paths, and uploaded clips. Lives on other hosting with Stripe; linked from one slot (`PRO_URL`).

**v1 deliberately won't do.** Host or upload video; download or re-host YouTube content; overlay anything on the player; accounts; analytics on viewers; quizzes or choice prompts; a native app; the new four-direction "file format" from the pitch-deck note.
