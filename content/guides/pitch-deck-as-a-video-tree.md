A slide deck answers questions in the order the founder chose. Investors rarely ask them in that order. A video pitch built as a tree lets each viewer pull on the thread they care about and come back to the story.

## The main track: the two-minute version

Record (or reuse) short videos for the core story: problem, solution, why now, traction, the ask. Upload them to YouTube as unlisted or public videos with embedding allowed. These five clips are the main track.

## Deeper tracks: the questions you always get

Under each main clip, add the answer to the question it invites:

- Under **solution**: a product demo.
- Under **why now**: the market shift, with the evidence.
- Under **traction**: how you measured it, and the cohort chart.
- Under **the ask**: use of funds.

Investors who are convinced keep swiping down. The sceptical ones go deeper exactly where they're sceptical.

## Practical notes

- **Unlisted videos work**, as long as embedding is allowed, so the pitch isn't public on your channel. Anyone with the rabbit hole's link can still watch them.
- **Keep each clip under two minutes.** Trim longer recordings with start and end times.
- **Send one link.** The whole tree is inside it; there's nothing to log into.
