Text FAQs are easy to skim but bad at showing things. Video answers show things but are hard to navigate. A branching path does both: each main clip answers a common question, and the follow-up questions sit one swipe deeper.

## Structure

- **Main track:** one clip per top-level question, in the order people usually ask them. "How do I set up?", "How do I invite my team?", "How does billing work?"
- **Deeper tracks:** the follow-ups. Under "How do I set up?": "What if I'm on Windows?", "How do I import old data?"

## Recording tips

- Answer the question in the first ten seconds, then show how.
- Keep each answer under ninety seconds; if it runs longer, split it into a main answer and a deeper "in detail" clip.
- Upload to YouTube as unlisted with embedding allowed if you don't want the clips on your public channel.

## Keeping it current

When the product changes, record the clip again, paste the new link into the builder in place of the old one, and replace the embed code on your help page. Old links keep pointing at the old version, so update every place you shared it.
